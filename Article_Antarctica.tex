\subsection{2.1 Daisy formulas} %creates a subsection

It started with the Daisy world model to describe the growth of the flowers in Antarctica.

\[\frac{dV_{b}}{dt} = V_{b}\left( V_{g}\beta\left( T_{b} \right) - \gamma \right)\] %growth of plants with constant death

This is the equation that describes the change in the number of plants depending on the temperature. It assumes that the death rate is constant and that the growth rate changes based on temperature.

\[\beta\left( T_{b} \right) = \begin{cases} 1 - k\left( T_{b} - T_{opt} \right)^{2} & \text{if } \left| T_{b} - T_{opt} \right| < k \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}\] %the Growth function

This describes the birthrate, based on the temperature, as a fraction between zero and one. With $T_{opt}$ as the optimum temperature and $k$ as the maximum deviation from the optimum temperature. 

\[A = \alpha_{b}V_{b} + \alpha_{I}V_{I} + \alpha_{g}V_{g}\] %total albedo

A is the total albedo across the daisy world planet based on the two types of daisies and the ground.
